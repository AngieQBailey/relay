Editorial Relay Race

Five rounds. Seven minutes each. Every round you open a classmate's news release draft and leave comments through a different editor's eyes. Comments only... don't rewrite anyone's release for them.

Before class: make your link commentable

Your draft stays wherever you wrote it. The race only needs a link a classmate can open and comment on.

WHERE YOUR LINK GOES

Paste your share link into the Editorial Relay Race: Draft Links doc by Sunday, Oct. 4 at 11:59 p.m. It's linked in Canvas. Touch only your own row.

IN WORD / ONEDRIVE

  • Open your draft and click Share
  • Change "People you specify" to People in University of Missouri System
  • Set the permission to Can review
  • Copy link and paste it in your row of the Draft Links doc

WHY "CAN REVIEW"

Review mode lets your classmates comment and suggest, and nothing else. Nobody can rewrite your release, and every mark they leave is yours to accept or reject. Your file never moves... it stays in your OneDrive the whole time.

TEST IT

Open your copied link in a private or incognito window. A University of Missouri sign-in prompt is normal... every classmate has that login. A "request access" screen is the problem: it means the share setting is wrong, and your editors will hit the same wall in class. (Wrote your draft in Google Docs instead? Share it as Anyone with the link, role Commenter.)

How the rotation works

The roster is a lineup. In round 1 you edit the person one row below you. In round 2, two rows below. Round 3, three... and the list wraps around at the bottom, so nobody edits their own draft and nobody gets skipped. No sign-up columns, no bookkeeping. The board computes it.
